02How Propeller Works

Propeller provides a technology platform (“Platform”) through which users (“Users”) participate in certain actions and earn points (“Points”) that can be redeemed for certain rewards, including sweepstakes entries (“Rewards”). Actions typically involve certain fundraising campaigns or social good missions (e.g. signing petitions, subscribing to mailing lists, pledging to volunteer, etc.) for participating charities, not-for-profits, and professional fundraising entities (each a “Cause”). In order to sustain operations of the Platform, Propeller typically charges a small fee to any Cause that uses the Platform (the “Fee”). Together with payment processing fees, the Fee will never exceed 20% of total donations, so that the charitable disbursement is no less than 80% of donations.

Propeller does not directly receive funds donated to a Cause through the Platform. Each Cause page on which a User may donate will indicate the recipient of the donation and the entity that charges the User’s payment method, and donations are received as described on that page. Depending on the Cause, donations may be received directly by the Cause or by a receiving charity that grants funds to the Cause, in each case less any Fees that are directed to be paid to Propeller, which may be set up as an automatic deduction and payment to Propeller. Except in the event of fraud and a valid chargeback by a credit card issuer, all donations to a Cause are non-refundable. Any refunds that might be issued are done so at the sole discretion of the applicable Cause or the receiving charity, as applicable. Use of the Platform is governed by these Terms.
