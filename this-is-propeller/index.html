An engine for collective action

Our story

Fans show up for the artists, creators and moments they love. Propeller turns that energy into measurable impact.

Since 2015, Propeller fans have raised $27.4M for nonprofits and taken more than 15.4M actions, alongside hundreds of artists and a growing lineup of brands, events and creators.

It works because it starts with what people already care about. An artist or creator invites their fans in. Fans take action or give to a cause and earn their way to experiences and rewards they can’t get anywhere else.

Our team comes from music, nonprofits, digital campaigns and events. We know what it takes to get people to actually show up, and we build every campaign so showing up is easy, fun and worth it.

What we build around

Our approach

1

Meet people where they are.

Around shared interests, favorite creators, brands and live experiences. We bring causes into the places where people already connect.

2

Make it a collaborative effort.

One person’s contribution is a starting point. We build campaigns that bring those contributions together, giving more people a part in what happens next.

3

Always in motion.

Every campaign feeds the next: more fans who show up, sharper ideas, stronger partners, and causes that keep getting support long after the moment passes.
