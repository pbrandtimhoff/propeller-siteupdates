FAQs

How do I cancel my monthly donations?

You can cancel most recurring donations yourself through your Propeller Dashboard. You’ll see your active donations under “Premier Status,” along with an option to cancel each one.

If your donation is processed directly by a partner organization (such as HRC, Sierra Club, or Reproductive Freedom for All), it won’t appear in your Propeller Dashboard, and you’ll need to cancel with that organization directly:

  • HRC: Email partners@hrc.org and let them know your donation was made via Propeller. Any changes to your gift must be made directly through HRC.
  • Sierra Club: Email member.care@sierraclub.org.
  • Reproductive Freedom for All: Email information@reproductivefreedomforall.org.

Cancellations are processed before your next scheduled donation. Need help finding a donation? Email us at hello@propeller.la and we’ll point you to the right place.

Heads up: Once you cancel and your subscription term ends you will no longer have access to point rewards, be able to enter experiences with points, earn double points on all actions, or be notified of exclusive merch drops from your favorite artists. All contributions via Propeller are non-refundable.

Has the winner of the experience I entered been selected?

After the winner has been selected it takes a few days to confirm all the details so it can take approximately a week to announce the winner. Be sure to check our winners list for updates.

I live outside of the US, can I enter the experience sweepstakes?

Unless specified otherwise, all contests are open to everyone worldwide. Please check your local laws to make sure you are able to claim the prize should you win.

Where is my reward?

Many of our rewards take at least 2–4 weeks to ship, please check the time frame detailed on the website for your reward. If you think it has been longer than expected and you still don’t have your reward then please contact hello@propeller.la and be sure to send your receipt.

Can I have my donation refunded?

Unfortunately, all donations made through Propeller are non-refundable. Refunding a donation comes at an expense to the non-profit who will be out of pocket.

How can I track my order?

Items ordered through Propeller are shipped in many different ways and therefore tracking is not always available. Each item has its own shipping timeframe so be sure to check the item reward page for your order to check the expected delivery time. If you have not received your item within that designated time frame then please contact hello@propeller.la.

What is your shipping policy (timeframes, costs, etc.)?

All shipping costs are included with your donation. Each item has its own shipping timeframe so be sure to check the item reward page for your order to check the expected delivery time. If you have not received your item within that designated time frame then please contact hello@propeller.la.
