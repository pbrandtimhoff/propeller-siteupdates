01Site; Sweepstakes

Propeller Enterprises (“Propeller”) is a California benefit corporation that provides a technology platform offered through Propeller’s website (propeller.la) (the “Site”). The Site allows eligible organizations to reach and engage with current and potential supporters. As part of the operation of its Site, Propeller allows such organizations to sponsor certain Sweepstakes, either for their own benefit or for the benefit of the organization (a “Beneficiary”) listed on the Sweepstakes Page. Users may gain entries to Sweepstakes by engaging directly with eligible organizations through the Site.

02Applicability of Rules

Each Sweepstakes is hosted on a separate landing page (a “Sweepstakes Page”) and shall be subject to any additional Sweepstakes-specific rules as set forth on the applicable Sweepstakes Page (“Sweepstakes-Specific Rules”). Each Sweepstakes Page lists the sponsor(s) and Beneficiary of such Sweepstakes.

03Methods of Entry

  1. A User may gain entries for any Sweepstakes by any of the following methods:
    1. Take any action on the Sweepstakes Page.
    2. Redeem points earned on the Site. The number of entries gained for point redemption will be specified on the applicable Sweepstakes Page. Points may be earned on the Site by taking any actions stated on the Site, including a Sweepstakes Page, which may include, for example only, making a donation, watching a video, sharing content on social media, joining a newsletter, etc. (each, an “Action”). For each Action you successfully complete, you will receive the number of points indicated in connection with the Action. You may not complete a particular Action more than once for additional points, unless the Action expressly indicates otherwise. Points earned on the Site are not specific to any Sweepstakes; e.g., you may earn points in connection with an Action that appears in connection with a particular Sweepstakes (or no Sweepstakes) but redeem those same points for entries into any other Sweepstakes hosted on the Site. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary herein, points are fungible, have no cash, monetary, or other value, and are only redeemable for entries into Sweepstakes or other prizes as may be offered on the Site from time to time, subject to these Rules and any applicable Sweepstakes-Specific Rules. Propeller may discontinue the offering of Sweepstakes as well as the ability to redeem points for entries on any Sweepstakes Page at any time in its sole and absolute discretion. Propeller may also change the number of points earned by any Action or the number of points required to enter a Sweepstakes at any time in its sole discretion.
    3. Alternate Method of Entry. To enter a Sweepstakes without making a donation via the Alternate Method of Entry (AMOE), click on “here” in the last sentence of the “fine print” section or the first sentence of the “important details” section on the respective Sweepstakes Page. After being redirected, click “Receive Entries” to complete your request. Each submission will grant you 250 entries. California residents receive the maximum of 10,000 entries for a single submission. Any received entries submitted before the Sweepstakes has ended will be added before a winner is selected. You can receive a maximum of 10,000 entries. No account creation is necessary to participate via the Alternate Method of Entry.
  2. Unless otherwise stated in the applicable Sweepstakes-Specific Rules, each User is limited to ten thousand (10,000) entries per Sweepstakes (as applicable, the “Limit”). If a User submits entries to a Sweepstakes in excess of the applicable Limit, all of such User’s excess entries to that Sweepstakes will be void, regardless of the method(s) of entry.
  3. Any of the following will void all entries from a User and may result in such User’s disqualification from all Sweepstakes and/or suspension or termination of a User’s account:
    1. Use of any robotic, mechanical, automatic, programmed, or similar entry method, including without limitation submission of any copy, facsimile, or other reproduction;
    2. Entering more than the number of times permitted, including without limitation by using multiple email addresses, identities, or devices in an attempt to circumvent the Rules;
    3. Inaccurate, incomplete, or untimely entries, or entries that do not comply with these Rules;
    4. Tampering with or undermining the Sweepstakes or the entry process by cheating, deception, or any other unfair practices;
    5. Any violation of the Rules or Propeller’s Terms of Service.

04Eligibility

Sweepstakes are open only to persons who are at least the age of majority in their jurisdiction of residence (typically 18 years of age) at the time of entry and who are residents of the United States or Canada, unless otherwise stated in any applicable Sweepstakes-Specific Rules. Certain Sweepstakes may have additional age restrictions, such as requiring you to be 21 years of age to attend certain events or receive certain items that are offered as a prize. Please refer to additional details on the applicable Sweepstakes Page before entering a Sweepstakes. Prizes will not be awarded to ineligible entrants. Additionally, if the potential winner is a Canadian resident, she or he must correctly answer a mathematical skill-testing question to be eligible to be confirmed the winner. Employees of Propeller and any organization that chooses to publicly associate itself with a Sweepstakes, and any of their respective owners, affiliates, directors, subsidiaries, or franchisees, and their respective immediate family members, are not eligible to participate in any Sweepstakes. All federal, state, and local laws and regulations apply. Void where prohibited.

All sweepstakes are void where prohibited by law. Residents of the following countries are not eligible to enter and may not receive prizes: Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, and the Crimea, Donetsk, and Luhansk regions of Ukraine. This exclusion applies regardless of the method of entry or Sweepstakes-Specific Rules. In the event that a winner is determined to reside in a prohibited country or territory, that winner will be disqualified and an alternate winner will be selected. Propeller reserves the right to verify the country of residence of any entrant or winner and to disqualify any entry from a prohibited location. This list of prohibited countries reflects current US Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) sanctions and is subject to change.

Multiple Participants may not share the same email address. In the event of a dispute as to the identity of any Participant, the authorized account holder of the email address used to enter will be deemed to be the Participant. The “authorized account holder” is the natural person assigned an email address by an internet access provider, online service provider or other organization responsible for assigning email addresses for the domain associated with the submitted address. Potential Winners and/or Winners (as defined below) may be required to show proof of being the authorized account holder.

05Sweepstakes Periods

Unless otherwise stated, a Sweepstakes shall commence at 12:00:01 A.M. Pacific Time (“PT”) and end at 11:59:59 P.M. PT on the days specified on the applicable Sweepstakes Page (each a “Sweepstakes Period”). Propeller is the official timekeeper for all Sweepstakes.

06Prizes, Approximate Retail Value (ARV), and Odds of Winning

  1. One or more prizes, as described on the applicable Sweepstakes Page, will be awarded after the final day of the Sweepstakes Period of each Sweepstakes. Unless otherwise expressly indicated on the applicable Sweepstakes Page, no Sweepstakes is affiliated with or sponsored, endorsed, or approved by any manufacturer or provider of the prize for such Sweepstakes.
  2. The ARV for each prize is listed on the applicable Sweepstakes Page.
  3. The odds of winning any Prize will depend on the number of entries received for a Sweepstakes. Each entry shall be equally weighted. Neither Propeller nor any organization that has associated itself with a Sweepstakes makes any warranties concerning any prize.
  4. Certain prizes may include an experience component (such as access to artists, events, or other non-tangible benefits) that will be selected by the winner from options made available by Sponsor at the time of Prize Notification. Experience components are subject to availability, have no cash value, and are not included in the stated ARV for such prize. Sponsor reserves the right to modify, substitute, or limit available experience options at any time prior to winner selection. Entry into a Sweepstakes through any affiliated promotional page does not guarantee availability of any specific experience component featured on such page.

07Random Sweepstakes Drawings

At the end of each Sweepstakes Period, Propeller shall randomly draw one (1) potential winner (a “Potential Winner”) from all eligible entries for each prize in a Sweepstakes. The Potential Winner will be awarded the applicable prize pending the Potential Winner’s confirmation and/or verification of the Potential Winner’s eligibility and compliance with these Rules and any Sweepstakes-Specific Rules. Propeller shall use a computer-generated method of selection that is designed to ensure that each drawing is conducted completely at random.

08Award of Prize

  1. Potential Winners will be notified via telephone and/or e-mail within ten (10) business days of the close of the Sweepstakes Period (“Prize Notification”). Message and data rates may apply. To claim a prize, a Potential Winner must reply to Propeller within the time indicated in the Prize Notification, or Propeller may select an alternate Potential Winner at random from the remaining Sweepstakes entries.
  2. Before awarding a prize, Propeller may require a Potential Winner to furnish proof of identification and other proof of eligibility under these Rules and any applicable Sweepstakes-Specific Rules. A Potential Winner will also be required to execute and deliver a tax acknowledgement form and an Affidavit of Eligibility and Publicity Release, pursuant to which a Potential Winner will grant Propeller and its assignees certain rights to use the Potential Winner’s name, likeness, photographs, biographical information, and other publicity rights as specified therein. If a Potential Winner’s jurisdiction of residence prohibits the conditioning of participation in a Sweepstakes on consent and release regarding publicity, such Potential User must object to Propeller in writing during the Verification Period, in which case a Publicity Release will not be required as a condition of being awarded the prize. Proof of identification may include satisfactory proof that the Potential Winner is the authorized account holder of the email account associated with the winning entry. If multiple persons claim to be a Potential Winner, and a single authorized account holder is unable to be timely verified, the prize will be forfeited, and Propeller may select an alternate Potential Winner at random from the remaining Sweepstakes entries.
  3. Except if a Potential Winner’s jurisdiction of residence prohibits the conditioning of participation on Potential Winner’s submission to a background check, Propeller reserves the right to conduct a background check on Potential Winner as a condition of awarding any prize.
  4. Except where prohibited by law, regardless of whether a background check is performed, Propeller reserves the right to disqualify a Potential Winner from a Sweepstakes if Propeller determines in its sole discretion that Potential Winner’s receipt of a prize or publicity in connection with the Sweepstakes could reflect negatively on, or harm the business or reputation of, Propeller or any of its organization clients. You agree to reasonably cooperate with Propeller in connection with any such background check.
  5. Notwithstanding any other provision of these Rules, Propeller reserves the right to disqualify a Potential Winner if Propeller determines in its sole discretion that the Potential Winner’s participation in the fulfillment of a prize would pose a safety or security concern to any individual involved in the prize experience, including but not limited to performing artists, event staff, venue personnel, other attendees, or the Potential Winner. Grounds for disqualification under this provision include, but are not limited to: (i) the existence of a restraining order, protective order, or no-contact order involving the Potential Winner and any individual associated with the prize experience; (ii) documented threats of violence, harassment, or stalking directed at any individual associated with the prize experience, whether communicated through the Site, social media, or any other medium; (iii) prior ejection, removal, or banning from any event or venue operated by or affiliated with any party involved in the prize experience; (iv) a credible report from venue security, law enforcement, or artist management identifying the Potential Winner as a known security concern; or (v) any other verifiable information reasonably indicating that the Potential Winner’s presence at the prize experience would create a risk of harm to any person. In the event of a disqualification under this provision, Propeller may select an alternate Potential Winner at random from the remaining Sweepstakes entries.
  6. A Potential Winner must execute and return all documents within ten (10) business days from the date they are sent, or the prize may be forfeited (the “Verification Period”), and Propeller may select an alternate Potential Winner at random from the remaining Sweepstakes entries.
  7. If a Potential Winner is found to be ineligible or not in compliance with these Rules or any Sweepstakes-Specific Rules, declines to accept the prize, is unable to be reached by Propeller or does not timely respond to Propeller, or in the event that a prize or Prize Notification is returned undeliverable, the Potential Winner will forfeit the prize, and Propeller may, in its sole discretion, select an alternate Potential Winner at random from the remaining Sweepstakes entries. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in these Rules, Propeller may abandon or limit any efforts to award a prize to an alternate Potential Winner, in Propeller’s sole discretion, in which case the applicable prize will remain un-awarded.
  8. Allow at least 8-12 weeks (or in some cases longer, if specified on the applicable Sweepstakes Page) for delivery of any physical prizes, which will be mailed to the address provided by the Potential Winner after Prize Notification.
  9. The right to receive a prize is non-assignable, non-transferable, and no prize substitution, exchange, or cash equivalent will be allowed. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Propeller reserves the right to substitute a prize of equal or greater value in its sole discretion.
  10. Federal, state and local taxes, if any, are your sole responsibility. All winners of prizes valued at $2,000 or more will be issued an IRS Form 1099.
  11. If a Prize includes travel, all decisions regarding the itinerary will be made by Propeller at its sole discretion. All expenses not specified on the applicable Sweepstakes Page, including, but not limited to, baggage fees, ground transportation & fuel, meals, beverages, gratuities, incidentals, and taxes, are the sole responsibility of the winner. The winner and any travel companions are solely responsible for satisfying all legally required conditions of travel. The winner and any travel companions must travel on the same itinerary and from the same departure location. If the winner lives within a 150-mile radius of the location where the prize is to take place, Propeller reserves the right to provide ground transportation instead of airfare (if airfare is offered as part of the applicable prize).
  12. Any unused portions of a prize are not transferable or exchangeable and cannot be redeemed as cash.
