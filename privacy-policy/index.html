05Information we Collect Through Automatic Data Collection Technologies

When you download, access, and use the Service, we may use technology to automatically collect:

  • Usage Details. When you access and use the Service, we may automatically collect certain details of your access to and use of the Service, including traffic data, location data, logs, pages viewed, interaction with the Service, Site preferences, and other communication data and the resources that you access and use on or through the Service.
  • Device Information. We may collect information about your device and internet connection, including the device’s unique device identifier, IP address, operating system, browser type, mobile network information, and the device’s telephone number.
  • Location Information. The Service may collect real-time information about the location of your device.
  • Behavioral Tracking. We also may use these technologies to collect information about your online activities over time and across third-party websites or other online services. We do not honor “Do Not Track” signals.

The information we collect automatically is only statistical data and does not include personal information, but we may maintain it or associate it with personal information we collect in other ways or receive from third parties. Such information also may be capable of being associated with, or be linked, directly or indirectly, to a particular customer or household. The technologies we use for this automatic data collection may include, without limitation:

  • Cookies (or browser cookies). A cookie is a small file placed on the hard drive of your computer. We use essential cookies and analytics cookies.
    • Essential cookies are essential for the Service to function correctly. They are necessary to provide the Service and are used solely to carry out or facilitate the transmission of communications over the network. Without them, you may not be able to access various functionalities of the Service. Examples of these cookies include: (i) session cookies that keep you logged in to the Service; (ii) persistent cookies that save your preferences such as language; and (iii) cookies that remember items you have chosen to save or track. Essential cookies are automatically placed on your computer or device when you access the Service. It may be possible to disable them in your browser or device settings, but doing so may make the Service or parts of it inaccessible to you. Unless you have adjusted your browser or device setting so that it will refuse cookies, our system will issue cookies when you direct your browser or device to the Service.
    • Analytics cookies, or performance cookies, are used to track visitors to the Service and their user behavior. This data is used to improve the way the Service works and to enhance the user experience. For example, Google Analytics cookies are used to collect anonymous information, including the number of visitors to the Service, unique visitors, where visitors have come to the Service from, and the pages of the Service they have visited.
  • Web Beacons. Parts of the Service and our emails may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that permit us, for example, to count users who have visited certain webpages or opened an email and for other related statistics (for example, recording the popularity of certain content and verifying system and server integrity).

10Text Messaging (SMS)

Propeller operates two separate text messaging programs. Each has its own opt-in, and opting in to one does not opt you in to the other.

Winner trip messages. If you win a sweepstakes or experience, you may choose to receive text messages about your prize trip by entering your mobile number and checking the consent box in the winner portal. This is optional and is not required to claim or receive your prize. These messages relate only to your own trip, for example flight and hotel confirmations, itinerary changes, check-in reminders, and day-of coordination. Message frequency varies by trip and is typically a small number of messages around your travel dates. You can reply to these messages to reach your Propeller contact directly.

The mobile number you provide in the winner portal is used only to contact you about your trip. It is not added to any marketing list, is not used for promotional messages, and is not shared or sold to any third party. It is shared only with the messaging provider that delivers these messages on our behalf. If you have separately opted in to receive marketing or campaign text messages from Propeller, that is a separate program with its own opt-in, described below.

Marketing and campaign messages. Separately, you may opt in to receive promotional and campaign text messages from Propeller. These are governed by the choices described in “What Choices Do I Have?” and by the disclosure practices described elsewhere in this policy.

For either program, reply STOP to any message to opt out, or reply HELP for help. You may receive one final message confirming your opt-out. Message and data rates may apply.
