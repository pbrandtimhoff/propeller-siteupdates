01What Premier Is

Propeller Premier (“Premier”) is a paid membership in Propeller’s rewards program, operated by Propeller Enterprises, a California benefit corporation (“Propeller”). These Membership Terms supplement, and incorporate by reference, Propeller’s Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. If these Membership Terms conflict with the Terms of Service on a Premier-specific matter, these Membership Terms control; on all other matters, the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy govern.

02Your Donation and Who Charges You

Premier requires a recurring monthly donation of at least $11 to the Cause you select at sign-up. Each Premier membership is tied to the single Cause you select at enrollment; you cannot change the Cause of an existing membership, so to support a different Cause you cancel your membership and enroll again. Your donation is made to that Cause (less any platform Fee, as described in the Terms of Service); it is not a payment to Propeller, though Propeller or its affiliate Propeller Impact, LLC may receive a fee in connection with your donation to help sustain operations. Depending on the Cause you support, your payment is processed in one of two ways, and the checkout page identifies which applies:

  1. Causes supported through Our Change Foundation. For these Causes, Propeller Impact LLC charges your payment method and your donation is received by Our Change Foundation (“OCF”), a 501(c)(3) public charity, which grants funds to your Cause. OCF will provide any receipt or disclosure required by law.
  2. Causes that receive donations directly. For these Causes, the organization you are supporting charges your payment method directly and receives your donation into its own account. Contributions to some of these organizations may not be tax deductible.

The entity identified at checkout as charging your payment method is the “Charging Entity” for your membership, and the disclosures shown at checkout for your Cause control how your donation is received and acknowledged.

Fees paid out of donations are disclosed at the point of enrollment. Together with payment processing fees, such fees will never exceed 20% of total donations, so the charitable disbursement will be no less than 80% of donations after fees and payment processing. Your Premier membership donation may not be tax-deductible. Please consult your tax advisor.

03Automatic Renewal and Cancellation

Your membership renews automatically each month. By starting a membership, you expressly and affirmatively agree that the Charging Entity identified at checkout will automatically charge your payment method the monthly amount you selected, each month, until you cancel.

You may cancel anytime through your Propeller Dashboard. If your donation is processed by a partner organization (Human Rights Campaign, Sierra Club, or Reproductive Freedom for All), you must cancel directly with that organization. See our FAQ for current contact details.. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period; you will not be charged for subsequent periods. Cancellation stops future monthly donations and Premier benefits. Donations already made are non-refundable, and any rewards you have already redeemed are not clawed back. When you cancel, any unredeemed Premier rewards are immediately and irrevocably forfeited, and because redeeming points for rewards is a Premier member benefit, you will not be able to redeem points while your membership is inactive. Any points you have accrued remain in your account in accordance with the Terms of Service.

If the Cause you support leaves the Program, your recurring donation will end and your Premier membership will become inactive; you may reactivate it by supporting another participating Cause.

04Points

As a Premier member you earn:

  • 1,000 points when you enroll, one time per member (awarded once and not repeatable, including if you cancel and re-enroll);
  • 20 points per $1 you donate, including your recurring monthly donation and any one-time donations (a $11/mo member earns 220 points/month);
  • additional points for completing eligible actions, with Premier members earning 2x points on eligible actions.

“Eligible actions” are activities Propeller may designate as point-earning from time to time (such as the actions and missions described in the Terms of Service). Point values are set by Propeller in its sole discretion and may vary from action to action, including between actions of the same type, and may change over time. The points available for a given action are displayed on that action, and the value shown when you complete it applies. Changes to point values apply prospectively and do not reduce points already credited to your account. Points expire and are otherwise governed as stated in the Terms of Service (points expire one year after your last login; points are not property, are non-transferable, and have no cash value). As long as your Premier Memership is active your points will not expire.

05Redeeming Points for Member Rewards

Premier members may redeem points for member rewards offered through the Platform (for example, tickets, merchandise, and experiences). Member rewards are fulfilled on a first-come, first-served basis while supplies last. Member rewards are not sweepstakes or prize drawings. Per-reward limits, point prices, and availability are set by Propeller in its sole discretion and shown at the point of redemption, and each reward may have its own terms. Redemptions are final. Member rewards are separate from sweepstakes entries, which are described in Section 6.

06Sweepstakes

Points may also be redeemed for entries into sweepstakes offered on the Platform. Each sweepstakes is governed by its own Official Rules, which control eligibility, entry periods, entry limits, methods of entry, odds, and prizes. No purchase, payment, or donation is necessary to enter any sweepstakes, and paying or donating does not increase your chances of winning; each sweepstakes offers a free alternative method of entry as described in its Official Rules. If these Membership Terms conflict with the Official Rules of a sweepstakes, the Official Rules control for that sweepstakes.

07Eligibility and Conduct

Premier is available to registered users who are 18 years of age or older. Some benefits, rewards, and sweepstakes may be available only to residents of certain jurisdictions (for example, sweepstakes eligibility is governed by each sweepstakes’ Official Rules), and the tax treatment of donations depends on your jurisdiction. Bots, automation, multiple or fraudulent accounts, circumventing limits, and reselling rewards are prohibited and may result in forfeiture of points/rewards and termination, consistent with the points-revocation provision in the Terms of Service.

08Changes

Propeller may modify or discontinue Premier, its benefits, point values, or these Membership Terms at any time, to the extent permitted by law and subject to any notice required for recurring charges. Changes to point values apply as described in Section 4.

09Everything Else

Liability, indemnification, disputes, governing law, and privacy are governed by the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. These Membership Terms may be amended in the same manner provided in the Terms of Service for amendments thereto.
